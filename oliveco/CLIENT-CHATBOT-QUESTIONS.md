# Information needed for the Oliveco chatbot

Please provide confirmed, current answers. If something is unknown, mark it “not confirmed” so the chatbot can refer customers to your team.

We already have the company name, Facebook contact, the public phone number +95 9 797 536795 (checked on the official Facebook Page on 28 October 2026), 28 catalogue entries, product photographs, and some product names, pack sizes and label information. The Page does not list an email address. Please confirm your preferred customer contact details, business policies and fuller product details below.

## 1. Opening hours and contact details — highest priority

- What are the opening and closing times for each day, including weekends? Which time zone applies?
- Are physical shop hours, pickup hours and Facebook/customer-service hours different?
- What happens on public holidays or temporary closures? Who will keep these hours updated?
- What is the full shop/office address and map link? Can customers visit or collect orders?
- Which phone number, email, Facebook page, Messenger, Viber or WhatsApp links may the chatbot share?
- How quickly should customers expect a human reply? Which contact should it use for urgent order questions?

## 2. Delivery, ordering and payments — highest priority

- Which cities, townships or countries do you deliver to? Are any areas excluded?
- What are the delivery charges, estimated delivery times and order cutoff times?
- Is pickup available? Where, when, and with what notice?
- Is cash on delivery available? Which other payment methods are accepted?
- What are the actual steps to confirm an order after a customer sends an enquiry?
- Who confirms prices, delivery charges and stock? The current website lets customers prepare an enquiry list; it does not take payments or confirm orders.

## 4. Returns, cancellations and damaged deliveries

- Can an order be cancelled or changed? Until when, and how?
- Which returns or exchanges are allowed, and within how many days?
- Are opened products excluded? What proof of purchase is required?
- How should a customer report a wrong, missing, leaking, damaged or expired product?
- Who pays return delivery charges, and how are refunds handled?

## 5. Wholesale and business enquiries

- Do you sell retail, wholesale, or both?
- What are the minimum quantities, available bulk discounts and reseller requirements?
- Can customers request quotations or invoices? Which team handles these requests?
- Are prices tax-inclusive, and how long is a quotation valid?

- Who is responsible for updating hours, prices, stock, delivery charges and product facts?
- How often will these details be reviewed, and who approves changes?
- Is there an existing spreadsheet or document that should be the source of truth?
